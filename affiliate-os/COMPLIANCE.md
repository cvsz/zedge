# Compliance architecture

Not legal advice. Requirements the product enforces by design:
- **Affiliate disclosure:** every content item and every link carries a disclosure; publishing is blocked if the platform's required disclosure (from the policy registry) is missing. Use each platform's own paid-partnership / branded-content tool where one exists.
- **AI-generated content:** labelled where a platform or law requires it; the studio records which assets are AI-generated.
- **Claims and advertising rules:** no invented claims, discounts, scarcity, reviews or earnings; regional advertising rules are policy-registry entries per market (Thailand first, to be confirmed with local advice).
- **Copyright, trademark, music:** only assets with recorded rights; licence and expiry tracked.
- **Privacy:** data minimisation (see PRIVACY.md); lawful basis and notices are the operator's responsibility; PDPA/GDPR-style export and deletion supported.
- **Platform terms:** official APIs and OAuth only; no scraping behind logins, no fake accounts, no engagement manipulation, no CAPTCHA or rate-limit evasion.
- **Provider data:** use commission and order data only as the affiliate programme's terms allow.
