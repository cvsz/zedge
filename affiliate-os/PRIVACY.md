# Privacy

- Click tracking stores: slug, time, day, platform, coarse country, and a bot yes/no. **No IP address, no user agent, no cookies, no fingerprint.**
- Visitors can see exactly where a link goes (`/go/{slug}/info`) with its disclosure. Links are never cloaked.
- Conversion and commission data come from the affiliate provider in aggregate or per order as the programme allows; personal buyer data is not requested.
- Retention: raw clicks 13 months then aggregated; configurable. Audit entries per policy.
- Operators must publish their own privacy notice covering the tracking described here.
